A SMARTER WAY TO BEGIN

Don't chase every idea.
Choose one path.

Online income usually takes time, practice and consistency. A focused plan helps you learn faster and avoid wasting money on unrealistic promises.

01

Know your starting point

Consider your available time, internet access, skills, equipment and budget.

02

Learn one useful skill

Use free lessons and practice projects before paying for expensive tools or courses.

03

Create proof of your work

Build sample projects, a portfolio or a simple product that demonstrates what you can do.

04

Test, improve and track

Speak to potential customers, measure results and improve based on real feedback.
